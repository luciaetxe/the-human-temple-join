About the Association

The Human Temple is a non-profit association dedicated to reconnecting human beings with the wisdom of their bodies. We believe the body is a sacred vessel — a temple that mirrors nature, time, and the soul’s journey.

Founded with the intention of cultivating presence, inner transformation, and embodied awareness, we offer tools and spaces to explore stillness, movement, and community.

As an association, we are rooted in values of authenticity, simplicity, reciprocity and regenerative living — holding space for others to remember who they truly are.

What We Offer

Yoga Practice

Traditional Ashtanga Yoga in Mysore style and guided classes for all levels, rooted in breath and presence.

Retreats & Workshops

Immersive experiences that invite introspection, movement, and connection — often held in nature.

1-on-1 Sessions

Personalized support through embodied guidance, intuitive listening, and somatic tools adapted to your needs.

Community Circles

Shared spaces for conscious dialogue, ritual, music, and reconnection with others on a path of awareness.
